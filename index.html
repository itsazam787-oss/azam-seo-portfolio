Keyword research, competitor analysis, search intent and backlink opportunity research.

2. Optimize

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, content, internal links and indexation-related improvements.

3. Build

Relevant directories, Web 2.0 properties, citations and other off-page opportunities.

4. Measure

GSC and Semrush are used to monitor clicks, impressions, rankings, keywords, backlinks and referring domains.
